Our story

DecoJolly comes from a family that has been in Christmas manufacturing for 40 years. Our factory has been making glass Christmas ornaments since 1995, and today it is one of the largest makers of hand-painted glass Christmas ornaments in the world.

Everything in our catalog is made in our own factories. There is no trading company between the factory and you, so the price you get is the factory’s price.

Our U.S. offices are in Arizona and Florida. Together with our team in Asia, we cover U.S. West, U.S. East and Asia hours, so someone is always there to answer. We handle quotes, samples, import and delivery, so you deal with one company from the first question to the delivered order.
