Our story

DecoJolly grew out of a family with manufacturing roots going back to 2011. We know holiday décor from the production side: how it is made, packed and shipped.

Everything in our catalog is made in our own factories. There is no trading company between the factory and you, so the price you get is the factory’s price.

Our U.S. side is in Arizona. We answer quotes, handle samples and coordinate import and delivery, so you deal with one contact in your time zone.
