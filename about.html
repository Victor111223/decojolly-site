Our story

From the factory floor to U.S. retail

DecoJolly grew out of a family with manufacturing roots and direct factory relationships dating back to 2011. We know seasonal décor from the production side — how it's made, packed, and shipped.

Today we put that knowledge to work for American buyers: sourcing curated seasonal products, managing custom and packaging programs, and handling import and logistics from our base in Arizona. The result is a partner who understands both ends — the factory and the U.S. retail floor.

Where we're going

Building a seasonal décor supply chain

Our long-term vision is bigger than selling product. We're building DecoJolly into a supply chain partner that connects U.S. retail and wholesale buyers with quality Asian manufacturing — across sourcing, custom development, packaging, QC, and delivery.

That means dependable programs for buyers, and a U.S. market access point for the makers we partner with.
