How to order

Three steps to a quote. No account, no minimum to ask.

Getting a quote

  1. Build a quote list

    Tap Add to quote on anything in the catalog. Your list stays in your browser while you shop.

  2. Send it

    Open the quote list, add rough quantities and when you need it, and press send. It goes straight to our sales team.

  3. Get your numbers

    We reply during U.S. business hours with pricing, minimums, case packs and sample options.

After you say yes

  1. Sample

    We make a sample in 15 to 21 days. You approve the product, packaging and specs before anything goes into production.

  2. Production

    Production time depends on quantity and is agreed with you up front. You get production photos and QC checkpoints along the way.

  3. Delivery

    We coordinate freight and paperwork. We can deliver duty paid (DDP) to your door.
